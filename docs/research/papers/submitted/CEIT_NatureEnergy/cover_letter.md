# Cover Letter

Dear Editors of *Nature Energy*,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled  
**"Geometry-Driven Renormalization of Elastic Fluctuations in Constrained Hydrogel Microfluidics"**  
for consideration as an Article in *Nature Energy*.

This work demonstrates that geometric confinement systematically renormalizes elastic fluctuations in hydrogel-based microfluidic systems. By combining high-resolution experiments with a functional renormalization-group framework, we establish a universal scaling law that governs elastic response across multiple geometries. These findings introduce geometry as a fundamental control parameter in soft-matter elasticity under confinement, defining a new universality class relevant to energy-related microfluidic technologies.

We believe this manuscript will be of broad interest to the readership of *Nature Energy*, as it connects fundamental physics of renormalization with practical implications for hydrogel microfluidics and energy materials. The results are reproducible, statistically validated, and supported by open datasets and code.

All authors have approved the manuscript and confirm that it has not been submitted elsewhere. We have included supplementary materials, raw datasets, and reproducibility checklists to ensure transparency.

We respectfully request your consideration of this manuscript for publication in *Nature Energy*.  
Thank you for your time and attention.

Sincerely,  
Hoang Bui Dinh
