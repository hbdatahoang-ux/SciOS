# Communication Protocol: CEIT Adaptive Control

This document defines communication channels, reporting rules, and document sharing practices for the CEIT project.

---

## Channels
- **Email:**  
  - Official updates sent via committee email.  
  - Drafts and submissions tracked in [Publications](ca://s?q=Explain_publications.md).  

- **Meetings:**  
  - Weekly sync meetings every Friday (logged in [Notes](ca://s?q=Explain_notes.md)).  
  - Quarterly reviews for roadmap milestones ([ROADMAP](ca://s?q=Explain_ROADMAP.md)).  

- **Internal Chat:**  
  - Quick discussions and clarifications.  
  - Decisions must be logged in [Governance](ca://s?q=Explain_governance.md).  

---

## Email Rules
- Subject lines must reflect content clearly.  
- CC committee for all milestone-related updates.  
- Attach figures from [Figures](ca://s?q=Explain_figures.md) and datasets from [Datasets](ca://s?q=Explain_datasets.md) when relevant.  

---

## Progress Reporting
- Weekly updates logged in [Notes](ca://s?q=Explain_notes.md).  
- Quarterly reports aligned with [ROADMAP](ca://s?q=Explain_ROADMAP.md).  
- Major deliverables tracked in [CHANGELOG](ca://s?q=Explain_CHANGELOG.md).  

---

## Document Sharing
- All files stored in CEIT repository.  
- Metadata maintained in [metadata.yml](ca://s?q=Explain_metadata.yml).  
- Registry of IDs tracked in [Registry](ca://s?q=Explain_registry.md).  

---

## Principles
- **Transparency:** All updates logged and accessible.  
- **Consistency:** Use defined channels for communication.  
- **Accountability:** Committee oversight ensures compliance.
