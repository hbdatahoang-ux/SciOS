# Training Roadmap: CEIT Adaptive Control

This document defines the onboarding roadmap for new contributors, structured by weeks and months.

---

## Week 1 — Orientation
- Read [Notes](ca://s?q=Explain_notes.md), [ROADMAP](ca://s?q=Explain_ROADMAP.md), [Governance](ca://s?q=Explain_governance.md).  
- Study [ETHICS](ca://s?q=Explain_ETHICS.md) and [QUALITY](ca://s?q=Explain_QUALITY.md).  
- Register in [Contributors](ca://s?q=Explain_CONTRIBUTORS.md).  
- Attend first weekly sync meeting ([COMMUNICATION](ca://s?q=Explain_COMMUNICATION.md)).  

---

## Week 2 — Data & Code
- Explore datasets in [Datasets](ca://s?q=Explain_datasets.md).  
- Learn preprocessing workflow in [Analysis](ca://s?q=Explain_analysis.md).  
- Review scripts in [Code Checklist](ca://s?q=Explain_code_checklist.md).  
- Practice running bootstrap resampling and FRG derivations.  

---

## Week 3 — Benchmarks & Validation
- Study benchmark standards in [Benchmarks](ca://s?q=Explain_benchmarks.md).  
- Validate datasets against reproducibility thresholds.  
- Document results in [Notes](ca://s?q=Explain_notes.md).  
- Log contributions in [CHANGELOG](ca://s?q=Explain_CHANGELOG.md).  

---

## Week 4 — Figures & Publications
- Learn figure standards in [Figures](ca://s?q=Explain_figures.md).  
- Draft supplementary materials in [Supplementary](ca://s?q=Explain_supplementary.md).  
- Review publication workflow in [Publications](ca://s?q=Explain_publications.md).  
- Practice citation management in [Citations](ca://s?q=Explain_citations.md).  

---

## Month 2 — Integration
- Contribute to [Integration](ca://s?q=Explain_integration.md).  
- Participate in quarterly review ([Governance](ca://s?q=Explain_governance.md)).  
- Ensure compliance with [SECURITY](ca://s?q=Explain_SECURITY.md) and [SUSTAINABILITY](ca://s?q=Explain_SUSTAINABILITY.md).  

---

## Principles
- **Transparency:** All progress logged in notes.  
- **Reproducibility:** Code and data validated before publication.  
- **Accountability:** Committee oversight ensures compliance.  
- **Collaboration:** Communication channels used consistently.
