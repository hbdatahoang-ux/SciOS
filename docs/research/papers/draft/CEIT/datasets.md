# Datasets Catalog: CEIT Adaptive Control

This document catalogs datasets used in the CEIT project, including description, source, status, and license.

---

## D-0001 — Experimental Filament Imaging
- **Description:** Single-molecule fluorescence imaging of DNA confined in microfluidic channels.  
- **Source:** Lab-generated, raw TIFF images.  
- **Status:** Finalized.  
- **License:** CC BY 4.0.  
- **Notes:** Used in Figures 2–4.  

---

## D-0002 — Curvature Correlation Data
- **Description:** Tangent–tangent correlation extracted from filament centerlines.  
- **Source:** Processed from experimental imaging.  
- **Status:** Finalized.  
- **License:** CC BY 4.0.  
- **Notes:** Supports CEIT vs WLC comparison in Figure 5.  

---

## D-0003 — Synthetic Noise Dataset
- **Description:** Hybrid dataset combining experimental signals with synthetic Gaussian noise.  
- **Source:** Generated via `synthetic_validation.py`.  
- **Status:** Draft complete.  
- **License:** CC BY 4.0.  
- **Notes:** Referenced in SI-5.  

---

## D-0004 — Bootstrap Resampling
- **Description:** Resampled datasets (N = 5000) for robustness analysis.  
- **Source:** Generated via `bootstrap_resampling.py`.  
- **Status:** Finalized.  
- **License:** CC BY 4.0.  
- **Notes:** Referenced in SI-4.  

---

## D-0005 — Resolution Sensitivity
- **Description:** Imaging datasets at varying resolution for reproducibility tests.  
- **Source:** Lab-generated, downsampled images.  
- **Status:** In progress.  
- **License:** CC BY 4.0.  
- **Notes:** Referenced in SI-6.
