# Ethics Statement: CEIT Adaptive Control

This document outlines ethical principles guiding the CEIT project, ensuring reproducibility, transparency, and compliance with scientific standards.

---

## Reproducibility
- All datasets must be documented in [Datasets](ca://s?q=Explain_datasets.md).  
- Code tracked in [Code Checklist](ca://s?q=Explain_code_checklist.md) must pass validation before integration.  
- Benchmarks in [Benchmarks](ca://s?q=Explain_benchmarks.md) must be independently verifiable.  

---

## Data Integrity
- Raw data stored without modification.  
- Preprocessing steps documented in [Analysis](ca://s?q=Explain_analysis.md).  
- Synthetic datasets clearly labeled and separated from experimental datasets.  

---

## Transparency
- All decisions logged in [Notes](ca://s?q=Explain_notes.md).  
- Roadmap milestones tracked in [ROADMAP](ca://s?q=Explain_ROADMAP.md).  
- Publications in [Publications](ca://s?q=Explain_publications.md) must disclose methods, datasets, and benchmarks.  

---

## Compliance
- Licensing: All outputs released under **CC BY 4.0**.  
- Ethical approval required for experimental protocols.  
- Committee oversight documented in [Governance](ca://s?q=Explain_governance.md).  

---

## Principles
- **Accountability:** Contributors listed in [Contributors](ca://s?q=Explain_CONTRIBUTORS.md).  
- **Acknowledgement:** Support recorded in [Acknowledgements](ca://s?q=Explain_ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md).  
- **Integrity:** No data fabrication or selective reporting.  
- **Openness:** All supplementary materials available in [Supplementary](ca://s?q=Explain_supplementary.md).
