# Abstract: CEIT Adaptive Control

Confinement fundamentally alters the elastic behavior of soft matter systems, yet a universal framework has remained elusive.  
Here we introduce the **Constrained Elasticity and Invariant Tensor (CEIT)** framework, which treats geometry as a renormalization field for elasticity.  

Using high-resolution hydrogel microfluidics experiments, we demonstrate a **universal scaling law** across multiple confinement geometries.  
Classical worm-like chain (WLC) models fail to reproduce fluctuation spectra under confinement, while CEIT uniquely captures both **spectral features** and **scaling collapse**.  

Functional Renormalization Group (FRG) analysis validates the theoretical foundation, and bootstrap resampling confirms robustness against noise.  
All datasets, code, and benchmark configurations are openly released to ensure reproducibility.  

Our results establish geometry-driven renormalization as a universal principle in confined soft matter, with implications for biological filaments and engineered hydrogel scaffolds.
