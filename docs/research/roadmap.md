# Research Roadmap: CEIT Adaptive Control

This document outlines the research roadmap for the CEIT project.  
It defines milestones, timelines, and dependencies across experiments, datasets, and benchmarks.

---

## Objectives
- Establish universality of geometry-driven renormalization in hydrogel microfluidics.  
- Validate theoretical predictions via experiments and datasets.  
- Benchmark CEIT framework against classical and alternative models.  
- Publish reproducible results in high-impact venues.
