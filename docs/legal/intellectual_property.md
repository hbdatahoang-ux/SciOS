# Intellectual Property Policy

Version: 1.0

Project: SciOS (Scientific Cognitive Operating System)

---

# Overview

This document defines the intellectual property (IP) policy for the
SciOS Research Platform.

The purpose of this policy is to clarify ownership, licensing,
attribution, and protection of software, documentation, scientific
artifacts, research outputs, and other intellectual assets produced
within the SciOS ecosystem.

---

# Objectives

This policy aims to:

- Protect original research.
- Preserve author attribution.
- Encourage open scientific collaboration.
- Support reproducible research.
- Enable long-term sustainability.
- Ensure legal compliance.

---

# Intellectual Property Scope

The following materials are considered intellectual property within
the SciOS project:

- Source Code
- Documentation
- Architecture Designs
- APIs
- Scientific Papers
- Figures
- Tables
- Equations
- Datasets
- Benchmarks
- Models
- Workflows
- Notebooks
- Supplementary Materials
- Metadata
- Specifications
- Technical Reports

---

# Ownership

Unless otherwise specified, original work created for SciOS remains
the intellectual property of its respective authors.

Project maintainers coordinate and manage contributions but do not
automatically assume ownership of contributor-created content.

---

# Copyright

All original content should include appropriate copyright notices.

Example:

Copyright (c) 2026 Bui Dinh Hoang

Contributors retain copyright for their individual contributions unless
a separate agreement explicitly transfers those rights.

---

# Licensing

SciOS source code is licensed under:

Apache License 2.0

Documentation and research artifacts are generally released under the
same license unless a different license is explicitly stated.

Published papers may additionally be subject to publisher-specific
copyright or licensing terms.

---

# Research Artifacts

SciOS treats every research artifact as a first-class object.

Examples include:

- Figures
- Tables
- Equations
- Datasets
- Benchmarks
- Experiments
- Models
- Workflows
- Supplementary Materials

Each artifact should include:

- metadata.yml
- version information
- author information
- licensing information
- dependency information

---

# Publications

Research papers remain the intellectual property of their authors.

After publication, copyright and distribution rights may be governed
by agreements with journals or conferences.

Authors must comply with publisher requirements.

---

# Attribution

Proper attribution should always be maintained.

Attribution applies to:

- Authors
- Contributors
- Institutions
- External collaborators
- Third-party resources

---

# Contributor Rights

Contributors retain ownership of their original work.

By submitting contributions, contributors grant the project the right
to distribute those contributions under the applicable project license.

Contributor responsibilities include:

- Submitting original work.
- Respecting third-party licenses.
- Providing accurate attribution.
- Following project contribution guidelines.

---

# Third-Party Intellectual Property

SciOS respects third-party intellectual property.

External software, datasets, models, figures, and documentation remain
the property of their original owners.

See:

docs/legal/third_party.md

---

# Patents

Apache License 2.0 includes an express patent grant for contributors.

Patent ownership remains with the original patent holders unless
otherwise agreed.

Contributors should avoid knowingly infringing third-party patents.

---

# Trademarks

SciOS names, logos, and branding may be protected by trademark law.

Use of project trademarks must follow the guidance provided in:

docs/legal/trademark.md

---

# Research Integrity

Authors are expected to:

- Report results honestly.
- Preserve reproducibility.
- Avoid plagiarism.
- Cite previous work appropriately.
- Maintain scientific integrity.

---

# Data Ownership

Ownership of datasets depends on their origin.

Categories include:

- Original datasets
- Public datasets
- Licensed datasets
- Institutional datasets

Users must comply with all applicable licenses and regulations.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Models

Machine learning models may have separate intellectual property
restrictions.

Each model should provide:

- License
- Source
- Author
- Model Card
- Metadata

before redistribution.

---

# Protection of Project Assets

Project assets should be managed using:

- Version control
- Metadata
- Registry systems
- Artifact identifiers
- Backup procedures

to preserve integrity and traceability.

---

# Dispute Resolution

Intellectual property disputes should first be reported to the project
maintainers.

Where necessary, disputes may be resolved according to applicable laws
or separate contributor agreements.
