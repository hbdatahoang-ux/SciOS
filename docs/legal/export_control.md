# Export Control Policy

**Project:** SciOS (Scientific Cognitive Operating System)

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

# Purpose

This document describes the export control policy for the SciOS project.

SciOS is developed as an open scientific research platform.
The project is intended for lawful research, education, reproducible science,
and industrial innovation.

This document provides guidance regarding export regulations,
restricted technologies, and international collaboration.

---

# Scope

This policy applies to:

- Source code
- Documentation
- Research artifacts
- Datasets
- Models
- Benchmarks
- Scientific publications
- Binary releases
- SDKs
- APIs

---

# Guiding Principles

SciOS follows the principles of:

- Open scientific collaboration
- Responsible AI development
- Compliance with applicable laws
- Transparency
- Reproducible research

---

# Export Compliance

Contributors are responsible for ensuring that their contributions
comply with the export control laws applicable in their jurisdiction.

Examples include:

- Export Administration Regulations (EAR)
- International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR)
- European Union export regulations
- United Nations sanctions
- National export control laws

SciOS itself does not guarantee that any contribution is exempt from
export restrictions.

---

# Restricted Contributions

The SciOS project does not intentionally develop or distribute:

- Military weapon systems
- Offensive cyber weapons
- Malware
- Surveillance tools intended for unlawful purposes
- Export-controlled classified technology

If a contribution falls under export restrictions,
it should not be committed into the public repository.

---

# International Collaboration

SciOS welcomes international scientific collaboration.

Contributors should ensure that:

- collaboration complies with local regulations;
- no restricted technology is transferred unlawfully;
- institutional policies are respected.

---

# Artificial Intelligence

SciOS includes AI technologies intended for:

- scientific research;
- simulation;
- automation;
- reproducible experimentation;
- engineering workflows.

These technologies should be used responsibly and ethically.

---

# Publications

Research publications generated using SciOS should comply with:

- publisher requirements;
- institutional policies;
- applicable export regulations.

---

# Third-Party Components

Third-party libraries may be subject to their own export restrictions.

Users are responsible for reviewing:

- third_party.md
- individual software licenses
- vendor documentation

---

# Responsibilities

Project Maintainers

- Maintain public compliance documentation.
- Review contributions for obvious legal issues.
- Respond to reported compliance concerns.

Contributors

- Ensure their submissions comply with applicable law.
- Avoid contributing export-controlled material without authorization.
- Disclose known restrictions where appropriate.

Users

- Use SciOS in accordance with local laws and regulations.
- Verify export requirements before redistribution.

---

# Reporting Concerns

Questions regarding export compliance may be directed to the project maintainers.

Potential issues should be reported privately before public disclosure when appropriate.

---

# Disclaimer

This document does not constitute legal advice.

Users, contributors, and organizations are solely responsible for determining
their own legal obligations regarding export control and international technology transfer.
