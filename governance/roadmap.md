# Project Roadmap

Version: 1.0  
Status: Draft  
Date: 2026-07-09  
Authors: Governance Committee  

---

## Vision
The project aims to establish a reproducible, transparent, and collaborative framework for scientific research artifacts.  
Our long-term vision is to integrate datasets, notebooks, manuscripts, and review processes into a unified ecosystem.

---

## Milestones

### Q3 2026
- **[Repository Standardization](ca://s?q=Explain_repository_standardization)**: Define and enforce directory structure across all submissions.  
- **[Metadata Integration](ca://s?q=Explain_metadata_file)**: Ensure all artifacts include machine-readable metadata.  
- **[Review Workflow](ca://s?q=Explain_review_workflow)**: Formalize peer review process with reviewer notes, responses, and revision history.  

### Q4 2026
- **[Automation Tools](ca://s?q=Explain_automation_tools)**: Develop scripts for generating figures, tables, and exporting manuscripts.  
- **[Validation Framework](ca://s?q=Explain_validation_framework)**: Standardize notebooks for exploratory analysis and validation.  
- **[Submission Packages](ca://s?q=Explain_submission_package)**: Create templates for journal/conference submissions.  

### Q1 2027
- **[Publication Pipeline](ca://s?q=Explain_publication_pipeline)**: Automate transition from submitted to published artifacts.  
- **[Governance Policies](ca://s?q=Explain_governance_policies)**: Establish rules for versioning, lifecycle, and archival.  
- **[Community Engagement](ca://s?q=Explain_community_engagement)**: Open repository for external contributions and collaborations.  

---

## Dependencies
- Requires consistent use of `metadata.yml` across submissions.  
- Relies on reproducible notebooks (`exploratory.ipynb`, `validation.ipynb`).  
- Depends on governance committee approval for policy changes.  

---

## Risks
- Inconsistent adoption of standards across teams.  
- Delays in peer review cycles.  
- Technical debt from legacy artifacts.
