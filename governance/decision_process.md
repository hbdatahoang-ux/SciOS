Version: 1.0  
Status: Draft

---

## Purpose
This document defines the **decision-making process** for project governance.  
It ensures transparency, accountability, and consistency in how strategic and operational decisions are made.

---

## Principles
- **[Transparency](ca://s?q=Explain_transparency_in_governance)**: All decisions must be documented and accessible.  
- **[Inclusiveness](ca://s?q=Explain_inclusiveness_in_governance)**: Stakeholders should have the opportunity to contribute.  
- **[Accountability](ca://s?q=Explain_accountability_in_governance)**: Decision-makers are responsible for outcomes.  
- **[Efficiency](ca://s?q=Explain_efficiency_in_governance)**: Processes should minimize delays while maintaining rigor.  

---

## Process Steps

1. **[Proposal Submission](ca://s?q=Explain_proposal_submission)**  
   - Any member can submit a proposal via `governance/proposals/`.  
   - Proposals must include rationale, expected impact, and alternatives.  

2. **[Review & Discussion](ca://s?q=Explain_review_and_discussion)**  
   - Governance committee reviews proposals.  
   - Open discussion period for feedback from contributors.  

3. **[Decision Making](ca://s?q=Explain_decision_making_stage)**  
   - Decisions are made by consensus when possible.  
   - If consensus is not reached, a majority vote is taken.  

4. **[Documentation](ca://s?q=Explain_documentation_stage)**  
   - All decisions are recorded in `governance/decisions/`.  
   - Metadata includes date, participants, and rationale.  

5. **[Implementation](ca://s?q=Explain_implementation_stage)**  
   - Approved decisions are assigned to responsible teams.  
   - Progress tracked in `governance/roadmap.md`.  

6. **[Review & Revision](ca://s?q=Explain_review_and_revision_stage)**  
   - Decisions are revisited periodically to ensure relevance.  
   - Updates documented in `governance/revision_history.md`.  

---

## Roles
- **Governance Committee**: Oversees decision-making and ensures compliance.  
- **Contributors**: Provide input, feedback, and propose changes.  
- **Maintainers**: Implement approved decisions in codebase and documentation.  

---

## Notes
- Emergency decisions may bypass standard review but must be documented retroactively.  
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed before voting.  
- This process is reviewed annually and updated as needed.
