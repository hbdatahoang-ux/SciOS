---

## Purpose
This document defines the **roles, responsibilities, and expectations** of project maintainers.  
Maintainers ensure the stability, quality, and sustainability of the project.

---

## Roles
- **[Code Maintainers](ca://s?q=Explain_code_maintainers_role)**: Review, merge, and manage code contributions.  
- **[Documentation Maintainers](ca://s?q=Explain_documentation_maintainers_role)**: Ensure clarity, accuracy, and consistency in project docs.  
- **[Release Maintainers](ca://s?q=Explain_release_maintainers_role)**: Oversee versioning, changelogs, and artifact publication.  
- **[Community Maintainers](ca://s?q=Explain_community_maintainers_role)**: Engage with contributors, handle issues, and moderate discussions.  

---

## Responsibilities
- Review and approve pull requests following project guidelines.  
- Ensure compliance with **[release_policy.md](ca://s?q=Explain_release_policy_file)**.  
- Maintain traceability between artifacts (datasets, notebooks, manuscripts).  
- Document decisions in **[decision_process.md](ca://s?q=Explain_decision_process_file)**.  
- Support contributors by providing feedback and guidance.  
- Monitor repository health (tests, CI/CD pipelines, dependencies).  

---

## Expectations
- Act with transparency and fairness.  
- Respond to issues and pull requests within agreed timelines.  
- Collaborate with governance committee on strategic decisions.  
- Disclose conflicts of interest before voting or approving changes.  
- Rotate responsibilities periodically to avoid burnout.  

---

## Appointment & Rotation
- Maintainers are appointed by the governance committee.  
- Term length: 12 months, renewable.  
- Rotation encouraged to bring fresh perspectives.  

---

## Notes
- Emergency maintainers may be appointed temporarily for urgent issues.  
- Maintainers are accountable to the governance committee and community.  
- This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed.
